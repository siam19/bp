About Banglapunk

Banglapunk is more than a brand; it's a movement. Born from the chaotic energy of Dhaka's streets and fused with cyberpunk aesthetics, we craft apparel for the digital nomads and street samurais of tomorrow.

We reject fast fashion. We embrace the limited, the rare, and the raw. Our drops are temporal artifacts, meant to be collected and worn until the system crashes.

Return & Refund Policy

Due to the limited nature of our drops, all sales are generally final. However, we accept returns for defective items or sizing exchanges within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unworn with tags attached.

Shipping Information

We ship globally. Domestic orders (BD) typically arrive within 3-5 business days. International shipping times vary by zone. Pre-order drops will include estimated shipping dates on the product page.

Privacy Policy

We respect your data. We only collect information necessary to process your order and notify you of drops. We do not sell your data to third-party corpo-rats.

Terms of Service
